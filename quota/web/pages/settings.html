Settings

Changes apply as soon as you save. No restart needed.

Your budget

Your weekly tokens are daily hours times workdays, unless you set a weekly limit.
Working days

Days off

Holidays, leave and sick days remove that day's share from your weekly tokens. Enter hours to take only part of a day (a 2 hour appointment, say). Pick any date, past or coming up.

Time away from the computer

In-person meetings and org events you logged on the Today page. They count as work and use tokens. Remove a mistake here.

Reminders

Reminders show as a pop-up from your computer and as a banner on the Today page. If the pop-up doesn't appear, check your system notification settings for your terminal or Python.

What Quota records

Everything stays in a file on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. For anything personal, use Private mode (the switch in the top bar). It stops all recording and is always off when Quota starts.

Categories

One line per category, in the form Name: app, app, app. An app matches when its name starts with a keyword. Up to six categories. Anything that matches none is counted as Other.
